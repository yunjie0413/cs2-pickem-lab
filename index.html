WHAT THIS MODEL KNOWS

A starting point.
A formula you can improve.

Map strength blends an overall Elo rating with a map rating. A rating difference becomes a win probability; map probabilities combine into a series probability. The simulator then follows Swiss records, Buchholz tiebreaks and rematch avoidance.

Demo ratings are invented. This version does not learn from past matches or model roster changes. More runs improve numerical precision, not prediction quality.
